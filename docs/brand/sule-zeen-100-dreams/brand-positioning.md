# Brand positioning

## Company identity

**Sule Zeen**

**Trade infrastructure. Cybersecurity. Built in Nigeria.**

Sule Zeen is a Nigerian technology company operating across two practices: commodity supply-chain infrastructure and cybersecurity. ZeenTheCyber is its distinct cybersecurity product brand. The proposed Sule Zeen Studio will publish practical digital-safety media and learning resources for people building businesses online.

The company's technical scope gives the identity its substance: verifiable commodity records, financing workflows, security testing, detection and compliance evidence. Public copy should explain the relevant capability, its operating status and the problem it addresses.

## Brand relationships

| Name | Role | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sule Zeen | Parent company | Nigerian technology company with two practices |
| Supply Chain | Practice | Commodity supply-chain infrastructure |
| Cybersecurity | Practice | Offensive security, detection and response, and compliance engineering |
| ZeenTheCyber | Product brand | Cybersecurity product identity under Sule Zeen |
| Sule Zeen Studio | Proposed media and learning extension | Digital safety for people building online |

Studio draws on the two practices without adding a third technical practice. It needs an editorial programme and a commercial model of its own. ZeenTheCyber keeps its name, product materials and identifiable relationship to the parent company.

## Message hierarchy

Lead company pages with the two practices and their scope. Present product specifications as specifications until deployment evidence supports stronger language. Introduce Studio as a named initiative in development.

For the Studio application, lead with its audience and learning products. Use the parent company's work to explain the founder's relevant experience, supported by examples. Supply-chain material can inform later programming about transaction records and customer trust; the first series has a narrower digital-safety remit.

Retain the founder's mission as a statement of direction:

> Sule Zeen secures the infrastructure that trade runs on — and builds the digital rails that Nigerian commerce moves across.

## Studio offer

**Digital safety for people building online.**

The initial audience is emerging Nigerian creators, freelancers and small online businesses whose work depends on social accounts, customer messages and digital payments. Proposed products are screen-led explainers, downloadable operating checklists and facilitated workshops. Topics should answer a specific question: how to prepare account recovery, check a suspicious request, manage access or handle customer information.

The commercial model starts with free public material and tests paid workshops and licensed learning packs. Price, buyer demand and repeat use require validation.

## Editorial standards

Use precise descriptions, named tools where relevant and demonstrations that viewers can follow. Date technical guidance and identify the platform or version shown. Publish corrections when guidance changes.

Label commercial relationships and product ownership. Sponsors may support production but should not determine technical conclusions. ZeenTheCyber should appear only where relevant, with ownership disclosed and alternatives assessed on the same criteria. Protect participant and customer information in examples.

Avoid unsupported superlatives, promises of guaranteed safety and claims of scale without dated evidence. Prefer an observable result, such as completing a recovery checklist, to an abstract claim about empowerment or transformation.
