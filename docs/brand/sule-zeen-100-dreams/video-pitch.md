# Video application draft

Suggested duration: **60–90 seconds**, subject to the official application format. This duration is an editorial recommendation.

## Script

I'm [full name], founder of Sule Zeen, a Nigerian technology company working in commodity supply-chain infrastructure and cybersecurity. ZeenTheCyber is our distinct cybersecurity product brand.

I'm proposing Sule Zeen Studio: digital-safety media and learning for creators, freelancers and small online businesses.

Our first series would cover account recovery, suspicious requests, access permissions and customer information. Each short demonstration would include a checklist people can use in their own business.

My relevant experience is [one specific project and your responsibility].

The business model starts with free explainers, then tests paid workshops and learning packs licensed to organisations that train small businesses. We would disclose commercial relationships and keep control of technical conclusions.

Our proposed 90-day pilot includes twenty audience interviews, twelve explainers and two paid workshops. We would measure what participants can do afterwards, what customers pay for and what delivery costs.

Support from 100 Dreams would help fund that pilot and, if selected, establish the content business in the UAE under the programme's requirements.

I want to build a publication and learning business that people return to when they need to protect the accounts and information their work depends on.

## Production notes

Replace the experience field with a verifiable example before recording. Read the script aloud and trim for natural delivery rather than speeding up.

Show a sample checklist or demonstration only when it has been produced and reviewed. Use readable captions, clear sound and a direct presentation. Confirm the application duration and file format, and the founder's ability to undertake the stated UAE arrangements. The programme name identifies the application destination; it does not indicate selection or endorsement.
