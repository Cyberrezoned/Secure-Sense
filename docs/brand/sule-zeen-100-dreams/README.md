# Sule Zeen: company positioning and 100 Dreams proposal

Working edition · 17 October 2026

## Brief

Present Sule Zeen as a Nigerian technology company with two practices: commodity supply-chain infrastructure and cybersecurity. Retain ZeenTheCyber as a distinct product brand. Develop a commercially specific proposal for Sule Zeen Studio, a planned media and learning extension whose initial subject is digital safety for creators, freelancers and small online businesses.

The company description and the funding proposal serve different purposes. The company profile explains the technical business. The application proposes a content business drawing on that expertise, with its own products, audience and revenue tests. Studio remains in development.

## Programme identification

“1 Billion Dream” is taken to mean **100 Dreams**, run with the 1 Billion Followers Summit, the UAE National Media Authority and Simon Squibb. Press coverage of the 15 October 2026 launch is consistent with that reading. Confirm it against the application itself before submitting.

### Confirmed by press coverage

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Award | USD 10,000 to each of 100 recipients (USD 1m total) |
| Support | Six weeks of free business training and mentorship, plus an accelerator run with Squibb's network |
| Ecosystem | Access to the UAE business ecosystem, described as including *potential* residency and further support |
| Applicants | Worldwide; need not already be UAE-based |
| Business types | Creator-led businesses, production companies and digital media platforms |
| Applications | Expected to open **20 November 2026**; a waiting list runs on the initiative's site beforehand |
| Summit | **8–10 January 2027**, under the theme **“Content for Good”** |

### What the programme says it is selecting on

Reported selection weighs *“purpose, energy, authenticity and capability”*, the applicant's needs, and the uniqueness of the idea. The stated aim is to turn ideas into **sustainable businesses** and to *“provide knowledge, capital, support and community”*. One organiser framed the ambition as hoping one of the hundred becomes a unicorn within ten years.

This matters for positioning: the programme funds a **content business**, judged on purpose and authenticity, not a technology services firm. The applicant entity is therefore Sule Zeen Studio, with the parent company supplying founder credibility and subject-matter authority. The summit theme “Content for Good” is a direct match for Studio's remit — protecting people whose livelihood depends on their accounts — and the application should use that language deliberately.

### Not established

A closing date, formal eligibility criteria, equity or repayment terms, and whether a UAE company or trade licence is required. One report mentions assistance obtaining trade licences; another does not mention a licence requirement at all. Treat UAE establishment as **unconfirmed** rather than required, and verify before relying on it in the application.
