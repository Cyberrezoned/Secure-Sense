# Sule Zeen

**Trade infrastructure. Cybersecurity. Built in Nigeria.**

Sule Zeen is a Nigerian technology company operating across two practices: commodity supply-chain infrastructure and cybersecurity. ZeenTheCyber retains its identity as a product brand under Sule Zeen. The proposed Sule Zeen Studio will extend the company's technical work into digital-safety media and learning for emerging creators, freelancers and small online businesses.

## Supply Chain

The commodity platform specification connects smallholder farmers, warehouses, merchants and financiers through a documented sequence from produce delivery to financing and settlement.

The supplied design describes farmers depositing produce at NCX-framework warehouses, with SEC-accredited warehouses participating in the network. Each delivery is graded and signed by an operator, then represented by a cryptographically verified Electronic Warehouse Receipt (e-WR). Merchants place offtake orders against these receipts; financiers underwrite the associated invoices; advances are disbursed to farmers through escrow.

A tamper-evident, hash-chained ledger records the workflow. The specification supports six roles through a mobile progressive web application and a web console, designed for low-connectivity Nigerian environments without blockchain or app-store dependency.

These are supplied product specifications. Warehouse participation and accreditation, deployed functionality, transaction activity and financing arrangements require supporting records before they are described as established operations.

## Cybersecurity

The cybersecurity practice covers offensive security, managed detection and response, and compliance engineering. Its stated scope includes penetration testing and red teaming across web applications, APIs, cloud infrastructure and identity systems.

The defensive architecture specifies Wazuh, Suricata and Sigma, with 24/7 detection engineering in the proposed service scope. Compliance work covers ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and NDPR, connecting control requirements to technical implementation, testing and evidence.

The supplied architecture aims for a fully open-source, independently auditable stack with no licence lock-in. That claim requires a component and licence inventory. Continuous service coverage requires operational evidence, including staffing, monitoring and response arrangements; naming the tools alone does not establish that capacity.

## ZeenTheCyber

ZeenTheCyber is Sule Zeen's distinct cybersecurity product brand. Its platform, tools and research resources retain their product identity within the company. Product materials should state the capabilities and availability of each offering separately from the broader service scope.

## Sule Zeen Studio

**Digital safety for people building online.**

Studio is a proposed media and learning extension of Sule Zeen's two practices. Its first intended audience is emerging Nigerian creators, freelancers and small online businesses.

The initial editorial programme would address account protection, suspicious requests, access management and customer-information handling. Short demonstrations would be accompanied by practical checklists. Workshops would give participants time to apply the material with guidance.

The proposed business model combines free public explainers with paid workshops and learning materials licensed to organisations. Audience demand, willingness to pay and production costs will be tested through a 90-day pilot. Sponsorship may be considered with clear disclosure and editorial control retained by Studio.

Studio is in development. The application describes proposed products and targets; no audience, revenue or achieved learning impact is established by this profile.
