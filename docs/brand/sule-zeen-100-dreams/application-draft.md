## Business proposal

I propose a media and learning business focused on digital safety for emerging Nigerian creators, freelancers and small online businesses. Its first products will explain how to prepare account recovery, recognise suspicious requests, manage business-account access and handle customer information.

The audience relies on digital accounts and transactions to work. Our research question is whether short demonstrations, reusable checklists and facilitated practice can meet a need that general online advice leaves unresolved. We will test this with interviews and sample material before committing to a full series.

The first content products are:

- **Account recovery pack:** screen-led lessons and a checklist for reviewing recovery settings, multifactor authentication and access permissions.
- **Suspicious-request guide:** worked examples of impersonation, payment-change requests and the checks a business can make before responding.
- **Customer-information workshop:** guided exercises on access, storage and routine handling of customer records, with take-home materials.

## Commercial model

Free explainers would introduce the material and show its quality. Paid workshops would provide facilitated practice and feedback. Learning packs would be offered for licensing to organisations that train creators or small businesses; these are prospective customers to interview, not existing partners.

The pilot will test who pays, which format they value and whether receipts cover the direct cost of delivery. Record paid registrations, refunds, delivery hours, licence enquiries and production costs. Prices will follow customer interviews and initial paid tests. Sponsorship is a later option, subject to disclosure and editorial independence.
