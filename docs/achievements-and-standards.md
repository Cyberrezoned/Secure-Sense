- OWASP mark: https://owasp.org/images/logos/favicon-owasp.svg, downloaded unchanged to `public/images/standards/owasp.svg`. Official branding reference: https://owasp.org/policy/branding-guidelines. Identifies OWASP ASVS; does not claim OWASP endorsement.
- ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, NIST CSF and CIS Controls use plain text references, not certification seals or recreated organisational logos. ISO restricts use of its logo (https://www.iso.org/iso-name-and-logo.html); PCI SSC does not provide a PCI DSS compliance logo (https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/faqs/1325/).

Official framework references are linked directly from the strip. No customer logos, client names, certification numbers or third-party endorsements were supplied or invented.

The strip provides a pause/resume control, pauses on hover, makes keyboard-focused references stationary and horizontally scrollable, and presents a wrapped static list for reduced-motion preferences and printing. Duplicate visuals are hidden from assistive technology and excluded from sequential keyboard focus.
